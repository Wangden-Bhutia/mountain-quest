How to play

  • ◀ ▶ to walk, JUMP to jump. Hold JUMP to jump higher.
  • Keyboard: arrows or A/D, Space/W/Up to jump, P or Esc to pause, M for sound, F full screen.
  • Bounce on grumps to beat them. Touching them from the side costs a life.
  • Cracked stones fall soon after you step on them. Pink springs throw you high.
  • Ice is slippery, ice teeth hurt, and wind streaks show which way you will be pushed.
  • Pink flags along the way are checkpoints. Your progress is saved after every chapter.

Install on iPhone or iPad

In Safari, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen. The game then opens from your home screen and plays offline.
